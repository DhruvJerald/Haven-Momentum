# Haven-Momentum

​GAME SLOGAN:

You must go lower to go higher!!

CONTROLS: 

A / D or ← / → — Switch lanes

SPACE — Slam down

DESCRIPTION:

• Bounce on the slabs to climb. Rise through a free lane, then slide over and land on top.

• Hit a slab from below and you'll bonk back down.

• Land on a NEW slab to get your full bounce back.

• Don't fall into the pit!

THE THEME

Every setback makes your comeback stronger. The more momentum you lose, the harder you can slam... and the higher you bounce back.
